# STORY.md: A Life in One Room

Working title. Competition theme: **Change**. Read this file with `DESIGN.md` (how it looks) and `SOUND.md` (how it sounds). If they disagree, this file wins on *what happens*, `DESIGN.md` wins on *how it looks*, `SOUND.md` wins on *how it sounds*.

> **Extended storyboard received (team):** `docs/storyboard_expanded.md`. How it fits and the open conflicts (Bird vs Bear, camera moves, the ending): `docs/storyboard_reconciliation.md`. Until the team decides, this file stays the contract.

## One sentence

One fixed room. No people. A whole life, from birth to death, told by the things in the room, which are alive. The person is never seen. They are felt through the door, the light, and how every object reacts. The film ends where it began: a new life.

## Format

- 60.0 seconds exactly. 16:9, 1920x1080. 24 fps, drawn on twos.
- Locked camera. No camera moves, ever. Change comes only from things, light, weather and time of day.
- No people. Faces only on the things the designer gave faces: the Teddy Bear (button eyes), the Lamp, the Mug and the Cushion (simple dot eyes and a small smile).
- No dialogue, no on-screen text, except an optional end title card after the last frame.

## Rules of this world

1. **The person is never shown.** When the door opens and warm light spills in, things perk up and lean toward it. When the door closes and the room is empty, they slump and breathe slowly.
2. **Things act with posture.** Lean, bounce, droop, tremble, squash. No talking.
3. **Nobody redecorates.** Things hop in, slide out, pack themselves into boxes, or pop in with a squash. The change is their own doing.
4. **One clock only.** Pencil height marks on the door frame. They climb through childhood and stop. In the family stage a second column starts and climbs. This is how the audience reads age.
5. **The warm light is the life.** Cold night plate, one warm light. It grows as a life grows and shrinks as it ends. See `DESIGN.md`, "Light is life".
6. **The pulse is 60 beats per minute.** One beat a second. The heartbeat, the music and the clock all keep it. See `SOUND.md`.

## The cast

Five characters carry the film. Everything else is ensemble.

| Character | Role | How it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **The Bed** | The shape-shifter. Carries every stage of a body. | cradle, crib, small bed, messy teen bed, double bed, single bed with rails |
| **The Door** | Every arrival and departure. The narrator. | Opens with light, slams, closes softly. Height marks on its frame. |
| **The Teddy Bear** | The first object and the thread through the whole film. Button eyes. | Two drawings: a **clean** bear when new (S1-S5), a **worn** bear with a stitched head and patched belly when old (S6-S8). It moves: shelf, high shelf, alone, into the crib, onto the cushion, floor again |
| **The Lamp** | Opens its eyes at the first cry, last to close them. | One tall floor lamp with a pull chain and a smile, the same lamp all film. Its light grows, dims and goes out |
| **The Window** | Weather, time of day, and the tree outside. | sapling, tall tree, huge tree. Sky and light change by stage. |

Living ensemble from the designer's sketches: the **Armchair**, the **Side Table** (curly legs that act as feet), the **Mug** and the **Cushion** (both with small faces). See `assets/items.md`.

Other ensemble (secondary, may be simple shapes): blocks, ball, mobile, boxes, posters, picture frames, wedding picture, plants, armchair, nightstand, glass, pills, cane, desk, guitar, wall clock.

## Ending: the loop (chosen)

The old heartbeat stops at 56.2 s. One second of true silence. The door opens on new gold light, the Teddy Bear waits on the floor, a faster heartbeat begins (120 bpm, a newborn), a new cry. The music's very first note returns at 58 s. Change is the end of a chapter, not of the room.

Alternative, not chosen: everything goes still and the film ends in silence and a title card.

## Hard rules for anyone generating material

- Do not add, move or remove anything that is not in the ledger.
- Do not change the window, door, shelves, floor line or bed slot. Ever.
- Do not show a person, a shadow of a person, a hand, or a face on anything except the Bear, the Lamp, the Mug and the Cushion.
- Keep every shot on the locked camera.
- Keep the stage timings. If a timing must change, change it here first, then in `SOUND.md`.
