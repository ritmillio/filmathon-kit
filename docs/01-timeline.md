# 01 · Timeline

The official schedule gives you roughly five hours: kickoff 17:30, **submission 22:30, no extension**.

| Time | Official | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 17:30 | Kickoff, theme reveal | Listen. Write the theme at the top of `docs/script.md` |
| 18:00 | Film language basics | Pay attention: this is the judging language (story, visual world, film language, sound, AI use) |
| 18:30 | Idea and screenplay | **One idea, one sentence.** Script + shot list done by 19:15 |
| 19:30 | Generation | Voice and music first (cheap, sets timing), then shots |
| 21:30 | Editing | Assemble with `rough_cut.py`, fix timing, listen on headphones |
| 22:15 | Export and submission | Export, run `check_delivery.py`, upload |
| 22:30 | Room closes | Already submitted by 22:15 |

## Rules of thumb

- **Cut scope, not quality.** 45 seconds of a tight film beats 90 seconds of filler.
- **Generate in parallel.** One person on shots, one on sound, one on the script/assembly.
- **Lock the script at 19:15.** Every later change costs generation credits and time.
- **Keep a spare.** Export a working film by 21:45 even if it is ugly. Improve it afterwards. Never be one failed render away from nothing.
- **Credits are finite.** The Pro package is 600,000 credits. Generate 2–3 variants per shot, not 10. Check your balance every 30 minutes.
