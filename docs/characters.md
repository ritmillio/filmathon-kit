# Characters: the continuity bible

**Draft compiled by the assistant from `STORY.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `assets/items.md` and the designer's sketches. The team approves it.**
How to use it: paste each character's **prompt block** word for word into every still or clip prompt where that character appears, and attach its reference image. Same words every time is what keeps a character consistent across 15 shots.

All characters share the look in `docs/shotlist.md` (STYLE) and the distance rules in `assets/references/figma/boards/characters/notes.md`: dot eyes and a small smile only, no lashes, no eyebrows, no hands, no costumes, scribbly brown outline, mostly passive.

## The Teddy Bear (the thread through the film)

- **Reference:** `assets/items/bear_new.png` (clean), `assets/items/bear_worn.png` (old)
- **Who it is:** the first object in the room and the last one standing. Quiet, loyal, a witness. It does not do anything. It is there.
- **Acting:** sits, tilts its head toward the cradle, slumps when alone. Never walks. It is carried and set down.
- **Key moments:** set on the sill with a soft plop (shot 2, 3.7 s); alone on the sill (shot 7); lifted into the crib (shot 9, 35.5 s); lies on the cushion (shot 13, 51.6 s); waits on the floor (shot 15).
- **Sound:** `plush_plop.wav`. Soft, cloth.
- **Prompt block, clean:** "a small hand-drawn teddy bear, round head with two small round ears, two black button eyes, a dark triangular nose, long thin arms and legs, sitting with one leg stretched out, flat brown fur with a lighter belly, thin warm-brown outline"
- **Prompt block, worn:** "the same teddy bear, old and mended: stitches across the top of its head, one eye replaced by a stitched cross, a stitched patch on its belly, a stitched seam down one side, slightly slumped, flat faded brown fur, thin warm-brown outline"
- **Do not:** give it a mouth, a smile, hands with fingers, clothes or a ribbon.

## The Lamp (the one warm light)

- **Reference:** `assets/items/lamp_floor.png`
- **Who it is:** the heart of the room. Its glow is the life: small at birth, strongest in the family years, trembling in old age, out at the end.
- **Acting:** opens its eyes at the first cry; blinks; rolls its eyes once as a teenager; leans toward the bed in old age; closes its eyes last.
- **Key moments:** eyes just opening (shot 2, 4.0 s); eye-roll (shot 6); glows warm (shot 8); leans over the bed, trembling (shot 13); eyes closed, glow out (shot 14, 56.0 s).
- **Sound:** `lamp_click.wav` (a pull-chain click), `lamp_hum.wav`.
- **Prompt block:** "a tall thin floor lamp, a trapezoid shade with a slightly tilted rim, a pull chain with small beads hanging from the shade, a thin pole, a small round base, two small black dot eyes and a small smile on the shade, flat cream shade, thin warm-brown outline, a soft warm gold glow when lit"
- **Do not:** give it arms, a larger face, lashes or eyebrows.

## The Mug

- **Reference:** `assets/items/mug.png`
- **Who it is:** a small, cheerful presence. A cup of tea is the sign that someone was here. **Proposed.**
- **Acting:** sits on the side table; a small tilt or a wobble; it is "looking up" at the bigger things.
- **Key moments:** beside the armchair (shot 8); on the side table in old age (shot 12).
- **Sound:** soft ceramic tick (use `wood_tick.wav` until a cup sound is added).
- **Prompt block:** "a small mug with one handle and a slightly crooked, tapered body, two small black dot eyes and a small smile, flat cream colour, thin warm-brown outline"
- **Do not:** add steam shapes with faces, a hat or arms.

## The Cushion

- **Reference:** `assets/items/cushion.png`
- **Who it is:** soft, sleepy, comforting. The bear lies on it in old age. **Proposed.**
- **Acting:** sags, puffs slightly, sleepy blinks.
- **Key moments:** on the teen bed (shot 5); under the bear (shot 13).
- **Sound:** a soft, muffled thump (use `plush_plop.wav` at a lower level).
- **Prompt block:** "a rectangular cushion with a small tassel at each corner, two small dot eyes with sleepy wavy lines above them and a small smile, flat pale colour, thin warm-brown outline"
- **Do not:** give it arms or legs (the tassels are only tassels).

## The Armchair

- **Reference:** `assets/items/armchair.png`
- **Who it is:** the dignified one. It blooms in the family years, wears in the quiet years, and moves to the bed in old age.
- **Acting:** no face. It acts by posture alone: it settles, sags, leans, and slides across the floor toward the bed.
- **Key moments:** appears (shot 8); looks worn (shot 11); slides beside the bed (shot 13, 50 s); goes still (54.6 s).
- **Sound:** `chair_creak.wav`, `slide.wav`.
- **Prompt block:** "a tall high-backed armchair seen from the front, a wide back that narrows slightly at the top, two small rounded arms and a flat seat cushion, short curved legs, flat warm colour, thin warm-brown outline, no face"
- **Do not:** add a face, hands or a cover with patterns.

## The Side Table

- **Reference:** `assets/items/side_table.png`
- **Who it is:** the small helper. Its curly legs act as feet. It holds the mug, the glass and the pills. **Replaces the nightstand.**
- **Acting:** no face. It shifts on its feet and settles.
- **Key moments:** with the mug (shot 8); with the glass, pills and mug (shot 12).
- **Sound:** a small wooden tick.
- **Prompt block:** "a small round side table on a single slender pedestal that curls into four curly legs with round feet, a flat round top with a thin edge, flat wood colour, thin warm-brown outline, no face"
- **Do not:** add a face or a tablecloth.

## The Bed (the shape-shifter)

- **Reference:** still to be sketched (cradle with hood, crib, small bed, single bed, double bed, plus a rumpled blanket and side rails).
- **Who it is:** it carries every stage of a body. It is always on the rug.
- **Acting:** no face. It "arrives" by popping in or sliding in, and "leaves" the same way. Its form is the passage of time.
- **Forms by shot:** cradle (2), crib (3, 9), small bed (4), messy teen bed (5), double bed (8, 11), child's bed (10), narrow single bed with rails (12, 13).
- **Sound:** `bed_creak.wav`, `slide.wav`, `pop.wav`.
- **Prompt block:** needs the sketches. Until then: "a simple wooden <form> with a soft blanket, standing on a striped rug, flat colours, thin warm-brown outline, no face".

## The Door (off-frame)

- **Who it is:** the narrator, never in frame. The door is the **light and sound** that fills the room, which lets us feel the person without showing them.
- **Acting:** warm gold light spills across the floor from the right edge when it opens; it closes with a soft click.
- **Key moments:** opens at 1.4 s (sound leads the picture); opens (11.4 s); slams (17.5 s); closes (25.2 s); opens warm (28.4 s); closes (41.6 s); opens with new gold light (57.3 s).
- **Prompt wording:** "warm gold light spills across the plank floor from the right edge of the frame". Never draw the door itself.
- **Open question:** the sketch has no door. This treats it as off-frame.

## The Window (weather and time)

- **Reference:** `assets/sketches/room_layout_sketch.jpg`
- **Who it is:** the world outside. It changes by stage: cold stars, dusk, blue rain, snow, warm evening, autumn leaves, pale dawn, white.
- **Acting:** nothing moves in the frame except the weather outside and, at the end, white light.
- **Prompt wording:** "a large four-pane window centred on the back wall with a wide wooden sill, <sky for the stage> outside"
