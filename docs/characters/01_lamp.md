# 01 · The Lamp

Same five headings for every character: **Base character · Base mood · Movement and behaviour · Personality · Key feeling**. Text marked *(team)* comes from `_source_karakterek_hu.md`. Text marked *(added)* is the assistant's proposal for the gaps and needs a yes.

Reference image: `assets/items/lamp_floor.png`. Prompt block and sound: see the bottom.

## Base character *(team)*
Cheerful, energetic, playful.

## Base mood *(team)*
Always smiling. Positive, enthusiastic, carefree.

## Movement and behaviour
- *(team)* Switches itself on and off.
- *(team)* Hops about on one leg.
- *(team)* Walks around the room.
- *(team)* Light, springy, playful movement.
- *(team)* Changes its place often and can hardly stay still.
- *(added)* Its single "leg" is the thin pole and round base: it hops on the base like a pogo stick, and the shade bobs and tilts like a head. No arms (see `docs/characters.md`).
- *(added)* Eyes and smile are on the shade. It switches on with a pull-chain click, and the chain swings when it hops.

## Personality
- *(team)* Always smiling. Optimistic and cheerful. Full of energy. Its presence spreads good mood.
- *(added)* It is the first to react to anything: when the door opens it hops toward the light before any other object moves.

## Key feeling *(team)*
Cheerfulness, playfulness, energy.

## The Lamp over a whole life *(added: this is the gap)*
The team notes describe the Lamp at full energy. The story needs the same character to grow old, because the Lamp's light is the life (`STORY.md`).

| Stage | Shots | Energy | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Birth | 2 | Waking | Opens its eyes at the first cry, one slow blink, one small bounce |
| S2 Childhood | 3-4 | Peak play | Hops on one leg, bounces whenever the door opens, switches itself on and off for the child |
| S3 Teens | 5-6 | Grumpy | Rolls its eyes at 18.2 s, sulks, hops less, light dimmer |
| S4 Away | 7 | Slumped | Off. Shade drooped. Barely moves |
| S5 Family | 8-9 | Radiant | Biggest glow, springy again, a happy double hop |
| S6 Quiet years | 10-11 | Easy | Gentle sway, hops rarely, glow slightly dimmer |
| S7 Old | 12-13 | Slow | Leans over the bed, trembles, one slow careful step |
| S8 End | 14 | Last | Closes its eyes, clicks itself off at 56.0 s |

## Conflicts with earlier notes: team to decide
1. **Walking around vs a locked room.** `STORY.md` fixes the Lamp at floor slot 1 (front left) in every shot. Proposal: it starts and ends each shot at its slot, and may hop or step within the shot. A new slot per shot would break the continuity ledger.
2. **"Changes its place often"** and the camera is locked. Proposal: movement inside a shot, never a long walk across the room (long walks drift in AI video).

## Prompt block
"a tall thin floor lamp, a trapezoid shade with a slightly tilted rim, a pull chain with small beads hanging from the shade, a thin pole, a small round base, two small black dot eyes and a small smile on the shade, flat cream shade, thin warm-brown outline, a soft warm gold glow when lit"

Motion words for clips (one at a time): "hops once on its round base", "the shade tilts and bobs", "blinks both eyes slowly", "the pull chain swings".

## Sound and music *(added)*

- **Musical voice:** bright, bouncy, plucked: xylophone or glockenspiel with pizzicato strings, a quick two-note hop figure for each hop. In old age the same figure on a slow celesta, with long gaps.
