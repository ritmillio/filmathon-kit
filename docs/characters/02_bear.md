# 02 · The Teddy Bear

*(team)* = from `_source_karakterek_hu.md`. *(added)* = assistant's proposal, needs a yes.

Reference images: `assets/items/bear_new.png`, `assets/items/bear_worn.png`.

## Base character *(team)*
Loving, patient, a little melancholic.

## Base mood *(team)*
A little sad. Quiet waiting and longing.

## Movement and behaviour
- *(team)* Mostly sits on top of the armchair.
- *(team)* Sometimes climbs down from the armchair.
- *(team)* Often climbs up to the window.
- *(team)* Looks out of the window a lot.
- *(team)* Watches the outside world and waits for its owner to come home.
- *(team)* Its movement is more restrained than the other characters'.
- *(added)* Small, careful movements only: a head tilt, a slow slump, a climb one step at a time. It never hops or runs.
- *(added)* Big moves (set on the sill, lifted into the crib) are done *to* it: "carried and set down", with the soft plop.

## Personality
- *(team)* Attached and loving. A little lonely. Waits patiently. Misses its owner.
- *(added)* It is the audience's eyes: when the Bear looks at the door, we look at the door.

## Key feeling *(team)*
Longing, love, absence, hope.

## Conflicts with earlier notes: team to decide
1. **The armchair.** The notes put the Bear "on top of the armchair", but the armchair first appears at 28 s (shot 8) in `STORY.md`. Options: (a) keep the sill for S1-S4 and use the armchair from S5; (b) bring the armchair in from the start. Recommended: (a), because the sill is the room's one fixed shelf and the Bear's "looking out the window" needs it.
2. **"Climbs"** vs "never walks" in `docs/characters.md`. Resolved here: it climbs and slumps in small steps but does not walk or hop.
3. **The two bears.** The clean bear for S1-S5 and the worn bear for S6-S8, the same bear, new and old. Still to confirm.

## Prompt blocks
- **Clean:** "a small hand-drawn teddy bear, round head with two small round ears, two black button eyes, a dark triangular nose, long thin arms and legs, sitting with one leg stretched out, flat brown fur with a lighter belly, thin warm-brown outline"
- **Worn:** "the same teddy bear, old and mended: stitches across the top of its head, one eye replaced by a stitched cross, a stitched patch on its belly, a stitched seam down one side, slightly slumped, flat faded brown fur, thin warm-brown outline"
- **Do not:** give it a mouth, a smile, fingers, clothes or a ribbon. Its face carries the sadness with the head angle alone.

Motion words: "tilts its head toward the window", "slumps slowly", "looks toward the door", "climbs one step up onto the sill".

## Sound and music *(added)*
- **Effects:** `plush_plop.wav` (set down), a soft cloth rustle (new: ask for "plush fabric rustle" when we do retakes).
- **Musical voice:** solo clarinet or warm cello, slow, a four-note lullaby theme with a yearning rise on the last note. It is the theme that returns at 58 s.
