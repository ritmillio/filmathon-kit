# 03 · The Mug (the Cup)

*(team)* = from `_source_karakterek_hu.md`. *(added)* = assistant's proposal, needs a yes.

Reference image: `assets/items/mug.png`.

## Base character *(team)*
Relaxed, rhythmic, full of life.

## Base mood *(team)*
Funky. Music, dance and enjoyment define it.

## Movement and behaviour
- *(team)* Wiggles all the time.
- *(team)* Enjoys the warm coffee sloshing around inside it.
- *(team)* Its movement is rhythmic, wavy and dance-like.
- *(team)* The coffee follows its body movement closely.
- *(team)* Loves its own little dance.
- *(added)* One idea per clip: the whole mug sways side to side on its base, the coffee surface tilts the opposite way and a few drops hop over the rim. Keep the coffee as a flat brown shape with a lighter rim, no foam art.
- *(added)* The handle is the hip: it leads each sway.

## Personality
- *(team)* Relaxed and free. Playful and cool. Likes to enjoy the moment. Rhythmic by nature.
- *(added)* It dances whether or not anyone is watching. That is why it matters in an empty room.

## Key feeling *(team)*
Funk, groove, ease, enjoyment.

## Conflicts and gaps: team to decide
1. **Where is the Mug before the family years?** Options: (a) it first appears at 28 s as in the story; (b) it is introduced earlier on the sill beside the Bear, so its groove is there for the whole film. A mug that grooves through the whole film adds joy, and a stopped mug in old age is a strong beat. Recommended: (b) only if a shot can hold it without breaking the room; otherwise (a).
2. **"Mostly passive objects"** in the character-distance rules (`assets/references/figma/boards/characters/notes.md`). The Mug dances all the time, which is the opposite. Fine: it is our own character, but keep the dance simple (side-to-side sway only) so it stays unlike the reference characters.
3. **Steam** must be plain wisps with no faces.

## Prompt block

Motion words: "sways side to side on its base, the coffee tilts the opposite way, a few drops hop over the rim", "slow tiny sway", "still".

## Sound and music *(added)*
- **Effects:** soft ceramic tick (`wood_tick.wav` for now), tiny liquid slosh (new, ask for "small ceramic cup, warm liquid sloshing gently, 1 second").
- **Musical voice:** upright-bass pizzicato with finger snaps and a soft brushed shaker, a loose walking figure. The score currently forbids drums; allow brush and snap only in the Mug's moments. In old age: the same groove at half speed, then a single bass note.
