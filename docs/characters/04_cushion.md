# 04 · The Cushion

Everything here is *(added)*: the team notes do not cover it yet. Needs a yes. Reference: `assets/items/cushion.png`.

- **Base character:** soft, sleepy, comforting.
- **Base mood:** drowsy contentment; a small smile and sleepy dot eyes.
- **Movement and behaviour:** slow and squashy. It sags, puffs back up, and does a slow sleepy blink. It never hops. It tumbles over softly when the teen bed is thrown about (shot 5). Under the Bear in old age it gives a tiny breath-like rise (shot 13).
- **Personality:** the comforter. It is where tired things rest. Gentle foil to the Mug's energy.
- **Key feeling:** comfort, rest, softness.
- **Shots:** 5 (on the teen bed), 13 (under the worn Bear).
- **Prompt block:** "a rectangular cushion with a small tassel at each corner, two small dot eyes with sleepy wavy lines above them and a small smile, flat pale colour, thin warm-brown outline". Do not give it arms or legs.
- **Sound:** soft muffled thump (`plush_plop.wav` at a lower level). **Music voice:** soft, low flute or muted horn, a long single note.
