## Why these three contrast (the craft)
Each character has its own **tempo and shape of movement**, so you can tell them apart in silhouette and in sound: the Lamp bounces (fast, vertical), the Mug sways (medium, horizontal, flowing), the Bear barely moves (slow, small). Together they make a small ensemble: joy, groove, longing. Keep the three tempos distinct in every shot.
