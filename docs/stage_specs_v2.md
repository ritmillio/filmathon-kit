# Stage specs v2 (redo from the team's revision brief, `docs/revision_brief_v2.md`)

Every stage has an existing v1 pair of frames to restyle: stage 1 = `stills/B2_start.jpg` + `stills/B2_end.jpg`; stage N (2-8) = `stills/sN_start.jpg` + `stills/sN_end.jpg`. Look at them first. The v1 clips are in `exports/`. **Keep the room, the layout and the cast the same** (see `docs/stage_specs.md` common rules: window centred with a sill, plank floor, striped rug, bed slot at the centre of the rug, lamp at the left, door on the right wall always visible). Change only what this file says.

## The new soft look (use these words in every still prompt)
"Hand-made drawing in soft wax crayon and coloured pencil on textured paper, gentle, delicate and slightly dreamy. Thin, soft outlines in a muted dark blue-brown, never black, about half as thick as before, slightly broken and feathered, not dominant. Gentle crayon grain, softly blended warm light, subtle torn-paper wall patches. Not vector, not polished, not glossy, not digitally rendered. Warm, tender mood. Limited palette: ultramarine, navy, cream, orange, ochre, brown."
**The lamp's eyes are always two small plain black dots**, hand-drawn with a slight imperfection: no pupils, no highlights, no outlines around them, no lashes, no whites. Only for a sleeping or switched-off lamp (stage 1 start, stage 8 end) may the eyes be two short soft curved dashes. Faces stay as designed: lamp (two dots and a small smile), bear (button eyes), mug (small face).

## Rules for the motion (use in every clip prompt)
- Smooth, continuous, physically connected motion on twos: gentle anticipation before every action, an arc, a soft landing or settle, follow-through. **No teleporting**: any object that changes place travels there in view along a path.
- **The lamp is one rigid piece**: the pole stays straight and upright (no bending, no bendy-stem stretching); it moves by hopping or tilting as a whole on its round base, the shade bobbing slightly.
- Doors, furniture, bed sheets and pictures move only when a stage says so. **The armchair, bed, cradle and sheets never move unless stated.**
- Locked camera, no zoom. No people (except the tiny distant silhouette in stage 4), no text, **silent**.

## How each stage is made
1. **Start frame.** Edit the existing v1 start still: reference image = that still. Prompt: "Redraw this exact picture ..." + keep every object and position the same + the new soft look + this stage's changes. Save the prompt to `docs/prompts/sN_v2_start.txt`.
   `python3 scripts/el_do.py still sN_v2_start --prompt-file docs/prompts/sN_v2_start.txt --ref stills/<v1 start>.jpg`
   **Look at the result** (Read the .jpg). If lines are still thick or black, the lamp's eyes are not plain dots, the layout moved, or something is wrong, redo it once.
2. **End frame.** Edit your v2 start frame into this stage's end state (`--ref stills/sN_v2_start.jpg`). Look at it.
3. **Clip.** Veo 3.1 standard (better acting than Fast): `python3 scripts/el_do.py clip sN_v2_std --prompt-file docs/prompts/sN_v2_clip.txt --start stills/sN_v2_start.jpg --end stills/sN_v2_end.jpg --model std --secs <6 or 8>`. The prompt = the look sentence + a STRICT CONTINUITY paragraph (what stays where) + the timed motion script + the rules above. See `docs/prompts/clip_B2_creative.txt` for the pattern.
4. **Check** 8 frames: `ffmpeg -v error -y -i shots/sN_v2_std.mp4 -vf "select='not(mod(n,18))',scale=480:-1,tile=4x2" -frames:v 1 stills/sN_v2_check.png` (for 8 s use `not(mod(n,24))`) and Read it. Failures: anything pops in or teleports, a stray character appears, the bed or door moves when it should not, thick or black outlines, lamp eyes not plain dots, the lamp pole bends. Re-run the clip **at most once** with a tightened prompt.
5. **Budget:** about 800 credits per still, about 7,000 per 6 s standard clip and about 9,500 per 8 s clip. **Hard cap: 30,000 credits for your stage.**
6. **Rules for you:** run generation commands in the **foreground** (they take 2-5 minutes; use a command timeout of 600000 ms) and **do not use background timers, monitors or polling loops** (they cause repeated notifications). Use only `scripts/el_do.py`. Never print or open the API key or `.env.local`. **Do not touch git. Do not edit any existing file**; only create your own `sN_v2_*` files. Silent. No studio or film names in prompts.
7. **Files:** `stills/sN_v2_start|end.jpg|png`, `shots/sN_v2_std.mp4`, `exports/sN_v2_silent.mp4` (copy of `exports/sN_v2_std_silent.mp4`), `stills/sN_v2_check.png`, `docs/prompts/sN_v2_start.txt`, `sN_v2_end.txt`, `sN_v2_clip.txt`.
8. **Report** briefly: the files, the checks (pass or the exact flaw), the credits you used.

For stage 1 use the file prefix `s01_v2` and the v1 frames `stills/B2_start.jpg` / `stills/B2_end.jpg`.

## Stage 1 (6 s): the room wakes up
Start: cold night room, lamp asleep (soft curved dashes for eyes), door on the right wall closed, bear seated on the sill, cradle at the centre of the rug. End: same room, door open with warm light, lamp awake with two black dot eyes and a small smile, glowing. **Motion:** 0-1 s the sleeping lamp breathes (shade nods slightly), the bear shivers once; 1 s the door swings slowly open on its hinges, warm light and sparkling dust spreading across the floor; 2 s the warm light touches the cradle (blanket flutters, a gentle rock) and the bear (ears perk, it sits up straighter and turns its head toward the cradle, **staying seated on the sill**); 3.3 s the lamp wakes: two dot eyes open, blinks, looks around, spots the cradle, crouches in anticipation, then hops once **in place** as one rigid piece with a soft landing, the shade glowing brighter, the pull chain swinging; 5 s settles.

## Stage 2 (8 s): childhood, a playful jump and a climb down
Start (from `stills/s02_start.jpg`): child's bed with the patchwork blanket at the centre of the rug, toys, crayon drawings on the walls, height marks on the door frame, door open with warm light, **lamp at the left on its base, lit, with two dot eyes**, **bear (clean) seated on the sill**. End: **the lamp has jumped across the room and now stands on the right side of the floor in front of the door** (right front of the rug, upright, still lit), **the bear is now in the child's bed**, sitting against the pillow with the blanket tucked over its legs, content; the ball and blocks wherever they ended up. **Motion (one continuous 8 s shot):** 0-1 s the lamp crouches slightly (anticipation), the bear shifts toward the edge of the sill; 1-3.5 s the lamp hops in two or three smooth playful arcs along the floor from the left to the right, each with a squash on landing and the chain swinging; meanwhile 1.5-6 s the bear turns round, lowers its legs, climbs down from the sill using the wall and the headboard, arm over arm, and slides carefully into the bed; 6-8 s the bear settles into the pillow, the lamp bounces gently once and stands still; door light warm throughout.

## Stage 3 (6 s): teens, rain, the slammed door
Start: messy teen bed centred, guitar against it, posters, toy box at the left, rain on the window, **door ajar with a thin line of light**, lamp at the left dim, **eyes = two plain black dots (shifted upward to read as an eye-roll), nothing else**, small bear far end of the sill. End: door shut, room darker, posters crooked, lamp's dots drooping low. **Motion:** rain runs down the glass; 1 s the lamp's dots drift up and round in a slow eye-roll; 2.5 s the door slams shut; 2.7 s a short gentle shake: posters flutter, the guitar wobbles slightly, the lamp tilts as one piece and rights itself, the bear bounces slightly; 4 s settles, dimmer.

## Stage 4 (8 s): away, the bear watches the door, then the window
Start: empty room (bare rug, boxes at the sides, faded poster rectangles, snow outside), **the bear (clean) sits in the exact centre of the rug facing the door**, looking toward the door; the **door is open** with a beam of warm light across the floor; the lamp at the left is off, shade drooped, eyes as soft dashes. End: **the bear now sits on the window sill looking out**; the door is **closed**, no warm light, cold blue-white light; through the window a snowy night with **a tiny, far-off dark silhouette of a person walking away along a snowy path, very small, no details**. **Motion (8 s, continuous):** 0-2.5 s the door closes slowly and smoothly, the warm beam narrowing and fading; the bear watches it, its head following; 2.5-3.5 s a long quiet pause, the bear's head sinks, its shoulders drop; 3.5-4.2 s it turns its head to the window; 4.2-6.5 s it climbs up from the rug toward the window in one continuous movement, paw over paw, onto the sill, never vanishing; 6.5-8 s it sits on the sill looking out; the distant silhouette grows smaller and fainter down the path; snow falls; gentle, melancholy.

## Stage 5 (6 s): family, a happy bear and a walking table
Start (from `stills/s05_start.jpg`): double bed centred, crib on the right of the bed with the star mobile and **the clean bear sitting in the crib, noticeably happier: bright button eyes, a small soft smile, relaxed posture, original face kept**; lamp at the left glowing, door open with golden light, the armchair at the right front, **the small round side table with curly legs standing next to the armchair, the smiling mug on its top**, wedding picture, plants. End: **the side table has walked across the floor to the left front of the room, in front of the bed's foot, the mug still on its top**; everything else unchanged. **Motion:** the bed sheet **stays still** (at most a very slight, slow breathing ripple from the sleeper; **no lifting, ballooning or jumping**); 0-1 s the mobile turns, the bear in the crib bounces slightly with a happy little wiggle, the mug sways; 1-5 s **the side table walks**: its curly legs step one after another in a rhythmic, slightly bouncy, whimsical walk, carrying the mug on top across the floor from the right front to the left front, the mug swaying gently but staying on the table top; 5-6 s the table stops and settles with a little bounce. The lamp stays upright and gently bobs.

## Stage 6 (6 s): quiet years, soft autumn dusk
Start/End from the v1 frames: double bed centred, worn armchair with the worn bear at the right front, the mug on its side table beside the armchair, framed pictures, autumn tree, a long beam of gold light on the floor, **door on the right wall CLOSED and it stays closed**. **Motion:** leaves drift past the window; the lamp (upright, rigid) sways very slowly; the mug sways slowly; the worn bear slowly turns its head toward the height marks; the beam of light slowly shortens and dims; nothing else moves.

## Stage 7 (6 s): old age, pale blue dawn
Start/End from the v1 frames: narrow bed with rails centred, the worn bear on the pillow, the armchair beside the bed, the side table with glass, pills and the mug, the cane by the door, the bare tree, the lamp near the bed, door closed. **Motion:** the blanket rises and falls very slowly; the lamp's glow trembles and flickers once and its shade tilts slightly toward the bed (as one piece); the bear on the pillow turns its head toward the lamp; the picture on the wall tilts slightly; **the armchair stays completely still** (no rocking, no tipping); the glow weakens slowly.

## Stage 8 (6 s): the end
Start/End from the v1 frames: the old-age room, white window, door closed, bear on the pillow. Start: the lamp glowing weakly with two plain dot eyes and a small smile. End: the room dark blue-grey (the window faintly pale), the lamp out, its eyes as two soft curved dashes, its smile still there. **Motion:** total stillness for a second; the lamp looks around slowly one last time; a gentle flicker; the glow slowly dims; one last flicker; it goes out and the room darkens **smoothly** (a clean fade, not black-out, **no sparkles, no yellow dots, no cable**); everything else stays still.
