# Stage specs v3 (second revision; `docs/revision_brief_v3.md`)

Builds on `docs/stage_specs_v2.md` (read it first: the soft look wording, the motion rules, the recipe, the budget and file rules). Same recipe: restyle/edit a start frame, **look at it**, make the end frame from it, **look at it**, then Veo 3.1 **standard** clip (`--model std`), check 8 frames (for 8 s clips use `not(mod(n,24))`), re-run the clip at most once. Use `scripts/el_do.py` only, run generation commands in the **foreground** (timeout 600000 ms), **no background jobs, monitors or timers**, never print or open the API key or `.env.local`, do not touch git, do not edit existing files, silent, no studio/film names in prompts. Report briefly: files, pass/fail per check with the exact flaw, credits used. Files use the prefix given in your task (`sNN_v3_*`).
All clips here are **8 seconds** (`--secs 8`). Veo habit to fight: it swings doors, bends the lamp pole, and tips furniture; say in the prompt that the lamp and furniture are fixed and rigid and that the door stays as in the first frame unless this spec says it moves.

## Cast reminders
The clean bear (button eyes, brown, original design) is young and playful; the **old bear** (S6, S7, S8) is the same bear aged: faded fur, stitched seam across the head, a stitched cross over one eye, a patch on the belly and one on the arm, a slightly drooping ear, loose threads, sagging tired posture; never frightening. Lamp: cream trapezoid shade, two plain black dot eyes (soft curved dashes only when asleep or out), small smile, straight thin pole, round base.

## S1 v3 (prefix `s01_v3`): the bear climbs down and looks into the crib
Start frame: edit `stills/s01_v2_start.jpg` only if needed (it is good: cold room, lamp asleep, door closed, bear seated on the sill, cradle at the centre of the rug). **Use `stills/s01_v2_start.jpg` as the start frame directly if it passes your look.**
End frame (new, edit the start frame): same room; the **door open with warm light**; the lamp awake and glowing (two dots, small smile); **the sill is EMPTY; the bear stands on the floor right beside the cradle (at its right/front side), leaning forward with its paws on the cradle's rim, head over the rim looking down into the cradle**; cradle and blanket unchanged.
Motion script (continuous, 8 s): 0-1.2 s still and cold, the lamp breathes asleep, the bear shivers; 1.2 s the door swings open slowly, warm light and a few dust sparkles spread across the floor; 2 s the lamp wakes (two dot eyes open, small smile) and glows, standing still and rigid; 2.2-5.5 s **the bear climbs down carefully from the sill**: turns round, lowers its legs over the sill edge, grips the wall and the sill with its paws, climbs down paw over paw and steps onto the floor (never falling or dropping, always in contact); 5.5-7 s it walks slowly across the floor to the cradle; 7-8 s it leans forward, paws on the rim, and looks into the cradle, head tilted, curious and attentive; the cradle rocks very slightly. Nothing falls into the room.

## S2 v3 (prefix `s02_v3`): the ball rolls out of the door; the bear never winks
Start frame: `stills/s02_v2_start.jpg` (child's bed centred, lamp at left lit with dot eyes, bear on the sill, ball and three blocks at the left floor, door open on the right wall with warm light). Use it directly if it passes.
End frame (edit the start frame): the lamp now stands upright at the right front near the door; **the bear is sitting in the child's bed against the pillow, blanket over its legs, BOTH button eyes normal and open, calm**; the three blocks remain at the left floor; **there is NO ball anywhere in the room and none visible at the door**; the doorway shows a strip of warm outdoor light.
Motion (8 s): 0-1 s the lamp crouches (anticipation) and the bear shifts at the sill edge; 1-3.5 s the lamp hops in two or three smooth arcs from left to right (the pole stays straight; the whole lamp hops as one rigid piece), meanwhile **the ball rolls steadily from the left across the floor, in front of the bed, toward the open doorway on the right wall, passes through the doorway and rolls out of sight, getting smaller; it never comes back**; 1.5-6 s the bear climbs down from the sill into the bed (as in the v2 clip, paw over paw, no falling); 6-8 s the bear settles against the pillow, eyes normal (no wink, no blink gesture), the lamp stands still and glows.

## S6 v3 (prefix `s06_v4`): the old bear gets out of the armchair; leaves only outside
Start frame (edit `stills/s06_v3_start.jpg`, which has the half-open door held still): keep the room, the double bed, the lamp, the pictures, the half-open door with soft gold light, the autumn tree and sunset outside; **change the armchair so the bear is fully visible**: the armchair at the right front angled toward the viewer and the room, **the old bear sitting upright on the seat IN FRONT of the backrest with its whole body visible (head, belly patch, arms, legs)**, visibly aged as described above, sagging tired posture; the little side table with the mug beside it unchanged; orange autumn leaves visible **only outside the window, behind the glass**.
End frame (edit the start frame): **the old bear standing on the floor beside the armchair**, hunched, one paw resting on the armrest, tired; everything else identical; leaves still only outside.
Motion (8 s): the leaves drift and fall slowly **only outside the window glass, never entering the room**; the door stays exactly half open, the lamp, bed and furniture stay still; the bear: 0-1.5 s sits tired, its chest rising slowly; 1.5-3 s pauses, slowly shifts its weight, puts its paws on the armrest; 3-5 s pushes up slowly, trembling slightly, stands on the seat swaying a little; 5-7.5 s climbs down over the armrest to the floor carefully, one limb at a time, pausing to rest between steps; 7.5-8 s stands hunched, catching its breath. Slow and stiff, never playful.

## ENDING v3 (prefix `s78_v3`): the final years, one continuous 16 s sequence made of TWO 8 s clips that share one frame
Two clips, `s78_v3_a` and `s78_v3_b`. **Make the shared frame E first** (edit `stills/s07_v2_end.jpg` or `s07_v2_start.jpg`): the old-age room (narrow bed with rails centred, the armchair beside it, side table with glass, pills and the mug, the cane by the door, the bare tree in a pale blue dawn window, the lamp near the bed with a weak glow and two dot eyes, door closed), **with the old bear lying on the pillow, the blanket tucked over its body, its head turned toward the lamp, eyes open, tired and gentle**. E is the END frame of clip A and the START frame of clip B (use the same file for both, so the join is invisible).
**Clip A** (start frame S = the same room, **the old bear standing on the floor at the foot of the bed, hunched, the pillow empty**, otherwise identical; end frame = E). Motion (8 s): the old bear stands for a moment, takes in the room (slowly turns its head to the window, the pictures, the lamp), then walks slowly and unsteadily to the bed, pausing to rest, climbs up the side rails carefully one limb at a time with pauses, crawls onto the mattress and lies back onto the pillow in a long slow settle; the blanket rises and falls gently; the lamp's glow trembles slightly and its shade tilts a few degrees toward the bed (the pole stays straight); the armchair stays completely still.
**Clip B** (start frame = E; end frame = the room dark blue-grey, the lamp out with its eyes as two soft curved dashes and its smile, the bear lying still, eyes closed). Motion (8 s): the bear slowly turns its head to the window and looks for a long moment at the pale light; lifts one paw a little toward the lamp and lets it rest; its breathing slows, the blanket rising less each time; the lamp's glow flickers once and dims; the bear's eyes slowly close; one last slow breath; stillness; the room darkens smoothly (a clean fade, no sparkles, no dots, no cable); gentle, quiet, reflective, nothing theatrical.
Check the join: the last frame of clip A and the first frame of clip B should look the same; if clip A ends differently from E, say so in the report.
