# Expanded storyboard: how it fits the project

Source (the team's, unchanged): `docs/storyboard_expanded.md`. This note compares it with `STORY.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `SOUND.md` and `docs/shotlist.md`, and says what the assistant adopted and what the team must decide. Until the decisions are made, the older files stay the contract.

## What it adds (adopted as ideas; not yet in the shot list)
- **The Lamp is the emotional protagonist**, with its own arc: energetic and curious, playful, protective, fragile. Fits `docs/characters/01_lamp.md`.
- **Scene 1 starts in the dark on the Lamp:** a click, a tiny warm light, two flickers, the eye opens, dust in the beam, the smile, a cry offstage, a little jump.
- **Object-to-object relationships:** the Lamp cheers up the Bear, the Bear watches the Lamp, the armchair and Lamp are companions in old age.
- **Match-cut transitions** between scenes: lamp light into the childhood room, spinning toy wheel into spinning mobile, bright drawing into its faded version, packed box into the new bed.
- **Richer props per stage:** toy car, train, paper aeroplane, football, books piling up, faded rectangles where posters hung, a forgotten toy in a drawer, a tilting picture frame.
- **The strongest image (Scene 7):** the Lamp's beam across the bed shows traces of the whole life (mobile outline, child's drawing, height marks, the Bear's shadow).
- **Emotional contrast:** first 22 s lively and funny, middle warm, last 22 s quiet.
- **Recurring-elements table** (Lamp, Bear, height marks, door, light, room).

## Conflicts: the team decides

## Decided by the team's feedback (21:30)
- **Continuity first:** nothing may pop in, vanish or jump to a new place within a shot. Objects that exist in a shot are in both the start and end frames.
- **The door is a real object on the right wall** (wooden, visible, opens and stays visible). This resolves the old open question (door off-frame).
- **The cradle (bed) stays at the centre of the rug.**
- **Look B** (marker, crayon, torn paper) is the film's look (`docs/style_v3.md`). No sound until the team does it.
