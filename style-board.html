How firm is this? The technique comes from press and Dior’s own description. The visual notes come from one full-size frame and three thumbnails. Colors are matched by eye, not measured, and frame rate and camera moves are not verified. Downloading the film for frame-by-frame measurement was not done.

Style DNA

One sentence: 1940s–50s European feature-animation craft, with flat cel-colored characters and thin tinted outlines over painterly textured backdrops, one warm light against a cold night, staged like theater.

Cut-out pipeline

Characters are drawn and animated separately, cut out, then assembled onto painted backdrops. This is the old multi-layer method, and it is the one we copy.

Painted plates

Night sky with broad visible brush streaks, pale snow drifts, paper grain. The ground is textured while the figures stay flat.

Tinted outlines

Lines are a darker tint of each fill, brown on gold, navy on a suit. Not black. Slightly irregular, handmade.

Caricature

Long noses, big hands, oversized eyes, small figures in big space. French bande dessinée line, not anime and not 3D.

Warm against cold

Ultramarine and navy night, snow whites, one gold light, string-light bokeh. Los Angeles scenes read warmer.

Theater staging

Long shots, little camera movement. Emotion comes from body language. In Chomet’s earlier work, atmosphere (weather, passing light) is added in compositing.

Live lab: build the look layer by layer

An original wooden bird, drawn with code. Switch each ingredient off and on to see what it contributes. Turn the frame rate down to 8 for the “drawn on twos or threes” feel.

Palette (matched by eye)

Click a swatch to copy its hex. These come from one frame, so treat them as a starting point.

Prompts and briefs

Constraint check. The final visuals must be AI-generated, with ElevenLabs as the only generation tool. Prompts 1 and 3 are for generating stills in ElevenLabs. Prompts 2 and 4 are art direction for our own sketches and code animatics.

What we can rebuild in four hours

ElementFeasible?How
Painted streaky sky and snowYesNumpy or canvas: directional streak strokes over a gradient, then grain. One image per scene.
Flat cel characters, tinted outlineYes, if simpleFill shapes, stroke in a darker tint. Original characters only.
Handmade line wobbleYesRe-jitter outline points on every drawing; hold each drawing for two frames.
Cut-out riggingYesWing, head and tail rotate on pivots. This is how the real film is built.
Warm light on cold nightYesAdditive radial glow, bokeh dots, final grade in ffmpeg.
Weather and light changeYesAnimate sky tint, snow density, cloud shadow. Also carries the theme “Change”.
Fluid human actingNoAvoid. Use an object or animal as the lead.
Hand-drawn feature-grade character animationNoThe Dior film took months. Do not chase it.

Draft shot plan for “The Last Stone”

A proposal built on concept A from the handoff (fixed camera, a wooden bird on a windowsill, ends on a new windowsill). Locked camera matches the theater staging and keeps the schedule safe.

  1. 0:00 – 0:10

    Spring morning, 1965. Soft warm light, new wooden bird, snow-free sill. Establish the locked frame.

  2. 0:10 – 0:20

    Summer. Light swings across the sill, curtains change color. Bird unchanged.

  3. 0:20 – 0:30

    Autumn dusk. Window light turns amber, a television glow appears in the room.

  4. 0:30 – 0:40

    Winter night. The full Dior-style night plate: navy, snow, one warm lamp. The emotional center.

  5. 0:40 – 0:50

    Dust and empty light. Wallpaper fades, the sill is bare except the bird.

  6. 0:50 – 1:00

    Cut to a new windowsill, a new morning. The bird is the one constant. Hold.

Four-hour order of work

0:00–0:30 style test from this lab · 0:30–1:30 six painted plates · 1:30–2:30 bird rig and loops · 2:30–3:15 compositing, light and weather changes in ffmpeg · 3:15–3:45 music and sound in ElevenLabs · 3:45–4:00 final export and check.
